AgriCare MM

AI plant-disease detection & crop calendar for Myanmar farmers — English + မြန်မာ

Design document: idea flow, system architecture, data model, API, and build roadmap

Prepared August 2026

1. Concept Overview

AgriCare MM is a mobile-first web app for farmers with two core features:

FeatureWhat it doesValue to the farmer
Plant Doctor
(disease detection)		 Farmer photographs a sick plant. AI vision identifies the disease and returns the diagnosis, cause, and treatment steps in both English and Burmese. Instant expert advice without traveling to an agriculture office; understandable in the farmer's own language.
Crop Calendar
(planting tracker)		 Farmer records what they planted and when (e.g. rice on 15 June). The app computes the expected harvest window, sends reminders, and keeps a year-by-year cultivation history. Never miss the harvest window; see past seasons at a glance to plan the next one.
Why these two together: the calendar tells the app what the farmer is growing and at which growth stage the crop is. That context makes disease detection more accurate (rice at tillering stage has different likely diseases than rice near harvest) and lets treatment advice reference the farmer's actual timeline.

5. Data Model

TableKey columnsNotes
users id, phone, name, village, preferred_lang (en|mm), created_at Phone + OTP login — most Myanmar farmers have a phone number but not email.
crop_catalog id, name_en, name_mm, min_days, max_days, stages_json Seeded reference data: rice 120–140 days, chili ~90, groundnut ~110, etc. Drives harvest prediction and growth-stage display.
plantings id, user_id, crop_id, variety, planted_on, expected_harvest_start, expected_harvest_end, actual_harvest_on, yield_amount, season_year, notes One row per field/crop per season. season_year powers the yearly history view.
diagnoses id, user_id, planting_id (nullable), image_url, disease_en, disease_mm, confidence, result_json, created_at result_json holds the full bilingual AI answer (cause, treatment steps, prevention). Linking to a planting builds a disease timeline per crop.

6. API Endpoints

MethodEndpointPurpose
POST/auth/otp · /auth/verifyRequest OTP, verify and receive JWT.
POST/diagnosesMultipart image upload (+ optional planting_id). Returns bilingual diagnosis JSON.
GET/diagnoses?year=2026Diagnosis history for the user.
GET/cropsCrop catalog for the "new planting" form (bilingual names).
POST/plantingsCreate planting; server computes expected harvest window.
PATCH/plantings/:id/harvestRecord actual harvest date and yield.
GET/plantings?year=2026Yearly cultivation history.
AI prompt contract (Diagnosis service): the backend sends the photo plus context ("crop: rice, planted 75 days ago, region: Ayeyarwady") and requires this JSON shape:

{ "is_plant": true, "disease": {"en": "...", "mm": "..."}, "cause": {"en": "...", "mm": "..."}, "treatment_steps": [{"en": "...", "mm": "..."}], "prevention": {"en": "...", "mm": "..."}, "severity": "low|medium|high", "confidence": 0.87 }

If is_plant is false or confidence < 0.6, the app asks the farmer for a clearer photo instead of showing a shaky answer.

PhaseDeliverableDefinition of done

(week 1)		Photo upload → bilingual diagnosis result screen (no login; anonymous session).A rice-leaf photo returns correct disease + treatment in EN and MM.
2. Calendar
(week 2)		Crop catalog, new-planting form, harvest prediction, timeline view.Planting rice on a date shows the correct expected harvest window and growth stage.
3. Accounts & history
(week 3)		Phone OTP login, yearly history dashboard, diagnoses linked to plantings.A returning user sees 2025 vs 2026 seasons side by side.
4. Polish
(week 4)		Push reminders, offline calendar (PWA), low-confidence retake flow, Burmese UI review by a native speaker.Demo-ready: full farmer journey from photo to harvest record.
Risks to watch:
  • Burmese quality — LLM Burmese is good but review agricultural terms with a native speaker; keep a glossary of corrected terms and inject it into the prompt.
  • Connectivity — rural data is slow; compress images client-side (~500 KB) before upload and cache the calendar offline.
  • Wrong advice — always show confidence and add a disclaimer to consult the township agriculture office for severe cases; never recommend specific pesticide dosages without a verified source.
